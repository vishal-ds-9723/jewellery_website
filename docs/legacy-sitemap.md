# Aavya Fine Jewellery: Site Map

## Purpose

A single-page editorial jewellery experience that moves visitors through the Aavya story: first impression, discovery, belief, making, personal visit, and an ongoing relationship.

## Primary Navigation

- **Home** (`/#top`)
  - Hero: Made to become a story
  - Collection preview
  - House story preview
  - Craftsmanship preview
  - Appointment call to action
  - Newsletter signup
- **Collections** (`/#collections`)
  - Bridal Edit
  - Signature Pieces
  - Everyday Heirlooms
  - Future route: `/collections`
- **Our Story** (`/#story`)
  - The Aavya origin
  - Billimora as place and influence
  - Jewellery as modern heirloom
  - Future route: `/about`
- **The Craft** (`/#craft`)
  - Old-world techniques
  - Considered materials
  - Made for your chapter
  - Future route: `/craft`
- **Contact** (`/#contact`)
  - Private appointment request
  - Newsletter signup
  - Future route: `/contact`

## Supporting Pages To Add Later

- `/collections/bridal` - bridal sets and wedding styling
- `/collections/signature` - statement earrings, necklaces, and sets
- `/collections/everyday` - lighter pieces for daily wear
- `/product/:slug` - product story, materials, dimensions, care, and enquiry
- `/about` - founders, Billimora atelier, and brand philosophy
- `/craft` - artisan process and responsible sourcing
- `/journal` - styling notes, wedding stories, and atelier visits
- `/contact` - showroom details, appointment form, shipping, and returns

## Recommended Visitor Journey

1. **Feel** - The hero establishes mood, place, and the promise of a lasting piece.
2. **Find** - The collection preview gives visitors a quick path to discover jewellery.
3. **Believe** - The story explains why Aavya exists and what makes it distinct.
4. **Trust** - The craft section makes quality, technique, and materials tangible.
5. **Visit** - The appointment section turns interest into a personal conversation.
6. **Stay** - The newsletter keeps the relationship open after the first visit.
