Our story

Jewellery with
a little more soul.

Aavya began with a simple belief: the finest jewellery should feel like it has always belonged to you. We look to the old streets of Billimora, the quiet drama of a wedding morning, and the women who make every room their own.

Our pieces bring the poetry of Indian craft into a modern wardrobe. Wear them often. Keep them forever. Let them gather stories.
