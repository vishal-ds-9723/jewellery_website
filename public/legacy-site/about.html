About
Us.

The journey of R.A. Parikh Jewellers began with a small outlet in Billimora offering traditional jewellery. After the success of this store, we opened a spacious showroom with traditional as well as trendy designs, becoming a preferred choice for Indian ethnic fashion and setting new standards of customer delight every day.
